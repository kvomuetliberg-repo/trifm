The real trifm.lua script and the real norns parameter, scale and utility libraries, running in a Lua 5.3 virtual machine in your browser. Grid, arc and norns are virtual; the sound is a browser port of the SuperCollider engine.

Starts the Lua script and the audio. The sequencer begins playing at 110 bpm.

Playing it

  • Grid rows 1–7: click to place or remove a note on that step. The bottom note row is the root, the top is the 7th scale step.
  • Grid row 8: algorithms 1–8, clock divisions, run/stop on the far right.
  • Hold gestures: Shift-click a key to keep it held. Hold run, then click a column to set the loop length, or click clr to clear. Shift-click run again to release it.
  • Arc: drag up/down on a ring, or scroll over it.
  • norns: drag or scroll the encoders, click the keys. E1 pages, E2 selects, E3 changes. K2 plays a note, K3 runs/stops.

What is simulated

        
Exact: the Lua script, norns parameters, scales, clock divisions, grid and arc LED output, the screen font. Approximate: the sound. The browser engine follows the same SuperCollider design (phase modulation, operator 3 feedback, envelopes, ladder filter), but the real engine on norns will sound somewhat different.
